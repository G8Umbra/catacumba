Welcome to Catacumba

One screen for your projects, repositories, folders and web pages, so you can find them fast and pick up where you left off.

  • Free, no account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Private. Everything stays on this computer. Catacumba sends nothing anywhere.
  • Read-only. It looks and opens. It never changes, moves or deletes your files.
